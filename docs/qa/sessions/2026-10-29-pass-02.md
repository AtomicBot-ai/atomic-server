**Status:** Complete — B07 in the product and B08 in test infrastructure confirmed.

## Charter

Explore workspace edits, cancellation, history transfer and multiple-client state with a fresh isolated server, real local models and browser interaction to discover unintended persistence, silent overwrites and broken recovery.

## Planned probes and oracles

1. Skills and assistants: create, edit, cancel, rename, duplicate names, enable/disable and reopen. Cancel must preserve the saved version; accepted changes must survive reload and be visible to another client.
2. Conversation history: archive, restore, export/import and navigation during work. User-visible actions must preserve messages and make their resulting location discoverable.
3. Settings: leave a dirty form, return, retry after failure, change independent fields in two clients. Unsaved state must not be silently applied; a successful save must not overwrite unrelated newer values.
4. Follow unexpected observations, minimize and repeat candidates, check B01–B06 for duplicates, then add stable IDs starting at B07.
